Tobi's Garage Support

Questions: support@lomelu.com

Purchases

What is Full Garage?

A one-time purchase that unlocks every vehicle, today's and those added later. There is no subscription and there are no ads. Six vehicles are free to play: the fire truck, truck, excavator, garbage truck, airplane and ship. The pale, sleeping vehicles on the map wake up with Full Garage. The purchase is in the parent corner, opened with the lock button in the top right corner of the map; children never see a price or a buy button.

Can my child buy something by accident?

The purchase is only in the parent corner, which asks you to read numbers written as words. For extra safety, on Google Play keep authentication for purchases set to "For all purchases through Google Play on this device" (Settings > Authentication). On iPhone and iPad, you can turn off in-app purchases in Screen Time or turn on Ask to Buy in Family Sharing.

How do I get my purchase on a new device or after reinstalling?

Install the app with the same App Store or Google Play account. In the parent corner, open "Full Garage" and tap "Restore purchases".

Is Family Sharing supported?

On the App Store, yes: Full Garage also unlocks on the devices in your Family Sharing group. On Google Play, in-app purchases are not shared with the family.

It says "The payment is waiting for approval".

With family approval at Apple (Ask to Buy) or a payment method that takes time, the vehicles stay asleep until the payment is approved. Once it is, they wake up by themselves while the app is open or the next time it starts.

I would like a refund.

On the App Store, request it from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com; Apple decides on the refund. On Google Play, request it at play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory, or write to us. The store took the payment, so your payment details never reach us.

Playing

Does the game need the internet?

Not to play. The game never connects to the internet on its own, so it works on a plane or in the car. Only purchasing and restoring Full Garage need the internet through the store.

There is no sound.

On iPhone and iPad, the game is silent while Silent Mode is on. Turn Silent Mode off and raise the volume. On Android, turn up the media volume.

How do I turn off the background music?

Tap the music note button in the top right corner of the map or the garage. Game sounds and voices keep playing.

Which language does the game speak?

Turkish or English, following the device's language settings. On devices set to another language, it speaks English.

Who voices the characters?

The character voices were synthesized by computer. They come with the app: nothing connects to the internet during play, and your child's voice is never recorded.

How do I stop my child leaving the game by accident?

The back and map buttons inside the game never close it: the back button returns to the garage and the map button to the map. A half-finished repair waits where it was until the app is closed. On the map, Android's back button does not close the game. To keep your child inside the game, you can use Guided Access on iPhone and iPad, or app pinning on Android.

How do I reset progress?

Open the parent corner with the lock button in the top right corner of the map, go to "Progress", tap "Reset progress" and confirm. The repair and loading counts and the stars are deleted and the background music is turned back on. The Full Garage purchase is not deleted.

Privacy and contact

Does the app collect data?

No. See the privacy policy for details: https://lomelu.com/privacy

Why does the app ask for an age range?

The app never asks your child's age. In some countries and states, the law requires apps to request an age range signal from the device's store account. For this, the app requests the signal when it starts: on iPhone and iPad only in regions where Apple marks it as required, on Android only when the Google Play account's country is the United States. Apple or Google may show a window for it. The answer is read on the device, is not stored, is not sent to us and does not change the game; if you choose not to share, the game works the same.

How do I contact you?

Write to support@lomelu.com. If you are reporting a problem, the device model, the system version and what happened help us most. There is no need to send your child's name or photo. We use your address only to reply to you.
