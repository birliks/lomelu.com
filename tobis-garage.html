Tobi's Garage

Build and fix trucks, planes and boats. For children aged 3 to 5.

Every vehicle has a problem

Vehicles roll into Tobi's garage with a problem. The fire truck has lost its ladder, the plane has no propeller, the ship's anchor is missing. Your child drags the missing parts into place. The vehicle opens its eyes, starts its engine and heads off to work.

A problem you can see

Each vehicle arrives with parts missing and says what is wrong. Grey outlines show where the missing parts go.

Fix it with a finger

Your child drags each part into place. No losing, no timers: a wrong part simply slides back. When a vehicle comes back, it needs more parts, sometimes scattered on the floor or packed in a crate.

Off to work

Every vehicle has its own job scene. The fire truck sprays water, the truck unloads its cargo, the excavator digs, the plane takes off, the submarine dives.

No reading needed

Every instruction is spoken. Each vehicle names every part as it snaps on: "Wheel!", "Ladder!", "Propeller!" After the job, the vehicle stands in the showcase and its type is named; tap a part and it bounces and its name is spoken. The game speaks Turkish or English, following the device's language settings.

Your child picks the next one

Land, air and sea: 12 vehicles free, 48 with Full Garage. Your child picks the next vehicle on the map, and repaired vehicles wait there with their eyes open.

The loading station

At the loading station on the map, your child loads Boxy the truck: red goes on red, triangle goes on triangle. Each name is spoken as the load drops into place. The loading station is free.

While playing, your child

  • Hears the names of parts: wheel, ladder, propeller, anchor.
  • Matches colors and shapes and hears their names: red, blue, circle, star.
  • Gets to know vehicle types: fire truck, ambulance, crane, submarine and more.
  • Sees everyday jobs: firefighting, farming, recycling, fishing.
  • Sees cause and effect: fit the part, the vehicle works.
  • Drags each part into place with a finger, using hand and eye together.

For parents

Buy once

The fire truck, truck, excavator, ambulance, police car, garbage truck, airplane, helicopter, hot air balloon, ship, sailboat and fishing boat are free. Full Garage is a one-time purchase that unlocks every vehicle, today's and those added later. No subscription, no ads. On the App Store, Family Sharing is on.

Behind a parental gate

The purchase and the privacy policy are behind a parental gate. Children never see a price or a buy button; locked vehicles appear on the map as pale, sleeping silhouettes.

No data collected

No account. We never ask for a name, photo or location. No ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting tools. Progress stays on the device and is never sent to us.

No internet needed to play

Play on a plane, in the car, anywhere. The Android version does not even request internet permission. Purchasing and restoring need the internet through the store.

It ends gently

Optional bedtime: after 3 or 5 vehicles, or 15 or 30 minutes, the vehicles fall asleep and play winds down gently, with no penalty. After 90 seconds without a touch, the garage falls asleep: music and animations stop.

Honest about voices

The character voices were synthesized by computer. They come with the app, and your child's voice is never recorded.
