Meet Tobi's Garage

Who we are

Lomelu is a small studio. We make apps for the youngest players, children aged 3 to 5, and we take our time with each one.

Every instruction is spoken, so there is nothing to read. There is no losing and no timer. Nothing in our apps asks a child for a name, a photo or a location.

Our first app is Tobi's Garage.

Meet Lomi

Lomi is Lomelu's little bird. The three orange feathers on Lomi's head are the three syllables of our name: lo-me-lu.

Lomi is curious and gentle, never in a hurry and never loud. That is how we want our apps to feel.

What we promise

No ads

There are no ads in our apps. Your child will not see promotions for other apps or products.

No data collection

No accounts, no analytics, no crash-reporting tools. We never ask for a name, photo or location. Progress stays on the device.

No internet needed to play

Play on a plane, in the car, anywhere. Purchasing and restoring need the internet through the store.

Buy once

A one-time purchase, no subscription. The purchase sits behind a parental gate: children never see a price or a buy button.

Tobi's Garage

Vehicles roll into Tobi's garage with a problem. Your child drags the missing parts into place; the vehicle comes to life and heads off to work. For ages 3 to 5. 6 vehicles free, 48 with Full Garage.
