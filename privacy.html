Tobi's Garage Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Tobi's Garage (Turkish name: Tobi'nin Garajı) is a vehicle repair game for children aged 3 to 5. It is developed by Lomelu. This page explains what information the app uses and how.

In short

  • The app does not collect personal data.
  • There is no account. The app never asks for a name, photo or location.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no crash-reporting tools.
  • No internet is needed to play. The app never connects to the internet on its own; it talks to the device's app store only to purchase, to restore and, where the law requires it, for an age range signal.

Information kept on the device

To let the game continue where it left off, the app keeps five small pieces of information on the device:

  • how many times each vehicle has been repaired and how many loads have been finished at the loading station,
  • whether the background music is on or off,
  • the bedtime limit chosen in the parent corner and the time the current bedtime began,
  • the time the studio logo was last shown at start-up (so that it is not shown again when the app is reopened shortly after),
  • whether Full Garage has been purchased.

To play its sounds, the app places copies of its own sound files in its temporary folder on the device. These are the same game sounds for everyone.

This information is never sent to us. If device backup is turned on, the operating system may include it in your own backup (for example your Google account or iCloud). We cannot access that backup.

Internet and permissions

  • The app makes no network requests of its own. When purchasing and restoring, the device's store app goes online; the app only asks it for the Full Garage product and whether it has been purchased.
  • The Android version does not even request internet permission. It requests two permissions, only for purchasing: Google Play billing and reading the network state (whether there is a connection; this is not permission to access the network). Google's purchase library requires them. The Android version asks for the age range signal described below only if the Google Play account's country is the United States; it learns the country from Google Play billing and does not store it.
  • Where the law requires it in some countries and states, the app asks the device's store account for an age range signal; this information is read on the device, is not stored, is not sent anywhere and does not change the game.
  • The app does not ask for access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.
  • The app has no external links, chat or sharing.
  • If a sound file is missing, the app has the device's own text-to-speech feature read a fixed game sentence. That sentence contains no information about your child.
  • If you allowed it in your device settings, Apple and Google may show us technical summaries about crashes and performance that do not identify anyone, in their own consoles. This is covered by the stores' own policies. The app contains no extra code for it.

Purchases

The app has a single purchase: Full Garage. It is a one-time purchase that unlocks every vehicle, today's and those added later. There is no subscription and there are no ads. Twelve vehicles are free to play.

The purchase is only available in the "For grown-ups" corner, behind the parental gate. Children are never shown a price, a buy button or anything urging them to buy; locked vehicles appear on the map as pale, sleeping silhouettes.

Payment is handled by Apple or Google; your payment details never reach us. The device's store app verifies the purchase. The app stores only a "Full Garage unlocked" flag on the device and never sends it to us.

With the same store account, the purchase comes back on another device, or after reinstalling the app, with the "Restore purchases" button. Family Sharing is on at Apple. On Google Play, in-app purchases are not shared with the family.

Children's privacy

The app is made for children and does not collect personal data from them.

  • Türkiye (KVKK, Law No. 6698): No personal data is processed through the app.
  • United States (COPPA): No personal information is collected from children under 13. So there is no collection that would require parental consent.
  • European Union and United Kingdom (GDPR, UK GDPR): No personal data is processed through the app. There is no processing that would require additional consent for children.

If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example by email), please write to us and we will delete it.

Deleting information

Deleting the app removes all of its information from the device. If backup is turned on, the operating system may restore this information from the backup when the app is installed again. You can delete the copy in your device backup from your backup settings.

Deleting the app does not delete the purchase: Full Garage comes back with the same store account through the "Restore purchases" button. The "Reset progress" button in the parent corner deletes the repair and loading counts and the music setting, not the bedtime setting or the purchase.

Because the app sends us nothing, we hold no records from the app about you. Apple or Google keep the purchase record under their own policies. If you emailed us, we will delete the correspondence on request.

Contact

If you email us, we use your address only to reply to you and never share it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will publish the new text on this page with a new effective date.

About this website
